# Research-document rules

- Separate evidence, inference, hypothesis, and future work. Preserve an
  unresolved disagreement rather than selecting a preferred conclusion.
- Do not use model memory as bibliographic evidence. Every literature claim needs
  a verified, resolvable source and must identify source type.
- Interpret statistical results at the run/seed level; label smoke evidence and
  inadequate samples plainly.
- Update `RESEARCH_STATUS.md` after a material finding. Keep detailed work in a
  task-specific research artifact rather than expanding status indefinitely.
- Explain material findings in `HUMAN_GUIDE.md` in language suitable for oral
  presentation, without concealing uncertainty.
