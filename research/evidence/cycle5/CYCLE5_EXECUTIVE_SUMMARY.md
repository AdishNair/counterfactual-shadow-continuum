## Question, evidence and method

Can asynchronous CSC maintain production cadence while sustaining comparable counterfactual evidence with bounded resources during continuous operation? Cycle 5 separates production health, evidence availability, retained-state counts, memory trends and storage/fault availability. Trust remains Decision D and learning remains BLOCKED.

The immutable series is `results/cycle5-async/local-20261006-v1`: 84 factorial runs, 28 cells and three independent paired seeds, each with 200 epochs and 20 excluded warmups; plus 3,000-epoch normal and 1,000-epoch one-worker severe-delay probes. H=6, production pacing 40 ms and evidence deadline 300 ms were frozen. Warm readiness and recycling were separate from the evidence deadline. Conditions include cold/warm K=1/K=2, K=0 baselines, delays and bounded CPU/memory/storage probes. K excludes the mirror, and traffic supports only two distinct alternatives.

All 86 scheduled attempts completed. Deterministic verification checked source/config/artifact identities, factors, seeds, summaries and **41,686 branch replays with zero mismatch**. Production/workload hashes matched within each main seed; there were no missing production epochs, production errors or observed unauthorized mutations in the main series. See `research/evidence/cycle5/cycle5_results_analysis.md`, `research/tables/cycle5_verification.json` and the complete loss tables. Software trajectory agreement is separate from timing equivalence and counterfactual validity.

## Runtime and evidence health

The final measured-source gate passed 66 tests in 141.372 seconds; its archive is `results/cycle5-gate/runtime-20261006-v2/`. Warm workers hydrate fresh state/model/config/events/actuator/trace objects, restore the RNG contract, validate metadata/environment and recycle after 100 tasks or 60 seconds. The cold fresh-process baseline remains available. A versioned metadata allowlist and explicit destruction on tested reset faults support correctness beyond output agreement alone.

Complete evidence requires the mirror and **all originally requested alternatives**, not merely the admitted subset. Normal warm K=1 accepted all 1,080 requested measured branches, without expiry or drops. Normal warm K=2 accepted 1,608 of 1,620; 12 dropped, none expired. Median complete records during production were about 24.6/s and 24.5/s respectively. Normal complete evidence age was about 42 ms from production commit. The long normal run had age p95 126.148 ms and p99 165.768 ms, including recycling and coordinator availability.

The 3,000-epoch normal run completed 2,978 of 2,980 measured requested epochs: 8,936 accepted branches, one expiry and three drops. One epoch had accepted but incomplete evidence. The severe one-worker run completed zero of 980 measured epochs: 1,207 expired and 1,733 dropped branches. Requested K=2, median admitted K=0 and completed K=0. This preserves a logical production stream by discarding evidence, but does not deliver useful counterfactual availability at that load.

Partial evidence is reported semantically as any accepted nonproduction subset without the full requested set. The frozen raw `record_status == PARTIAL` metric omits some mirror-only/alternative-only cases; a separately hashed deterministic supplement reports them without changing completeness or the raw archive. Cold cells had no accepted partial evidence. Warm K=2 storage semantic partial coverage reached 0.172 in one seed. Every incomplete epoch retains its reason set in `research/tables/cycle5_epoch_loss.csv`; historical Cycle 4 losses remain unchanged. Historical startup cannot be isolated from dispatch/import/transport overhead, and new terminal reasons are not causal decompositions of all scheduler/storage delays.

## Production, resources and storage

Near-target median cadence is insufficient for robustness: normal cold K=1/K=2 seed 1501 had run medians 129.592/146.090 ms; warm K=2 storage in that seed had median 291.945 ms and p99 800.589 ms. Full per-seed distributions and the run-order figure preserve early-run timing heterogeneity. Strict 40 ms interval overruns and cumulative drift are descriptive scheduling measurements, not validated missed-actuation guarantees. Storage, comparison, polling and instrumentation remain coordinator costs.

The CPU helper used one sustained unpinned process on a 12-logical-CPU host; the memory helper touched a bounded 64 MiB allocation; storage rewrote a roughly 4 MiB file with flush and no fsync. Achieved load was logged. These probes do not establish saturation of CPU, physical memory or disk. Windows current working-set RSS, Python allocations and process CPU are distinct; short CPU clocks remain quantized. Total complete-records/CPU-second efficiency is unavailable because unsuccessful-worker CPU is incomplete.

All-online second-half coordinator RSS slopes were **+339.915 and +265.009 bytes/epoch** in the normal/saturation probes; Python current-allocation slopes were **+23.340 and +191.497 bytes/epoch**. Maximum RSS was 37.816/35.563 MiB. These positive trends do not establish a stable memory region. The earlier warmup-excluded slopes remain separately reported. A 110-call diagnostic found all observed sampler `Counters` classes collectible after GC, rejecting that particular retention hypothesis within the probe; it does not explain all memory growth.

Append-only evidence reached about 125.186/15.321 MiB and grew with epoch count. No total archive quota/export mechanism exists. Exact end-of-run reduction loads full history after production; normal-long summary RSS reached **488.430 MiB**, distinct from live hot memory. The longest run is below the specification's **10,000 consecutive epochs at ≥90% completeness**, and factorial runs are below its 500-epoch condition standard. No full specification pass is claimed.

## Claims, open questions and Cycle 6

Upgraded claims are finite first-party worker equivalence/reset checks, measured normal-load evidence-yield improvement and finite retained ledger/ring counts. Logical async polling remains locally supported. Resource independence, uniformly robust cadence, indefinitely bounded memory/storage, durable recovery, physical validity, hostile containment, universal scalability and domain independence remain unsupported. The information timing audit is analysis only; the domain interface is proposed only.

**Cycle 6 recommendation:** continue systems work. First validate the reviewed transport/physical-queue fixes, separate required production durability from bounded speculative persistence, add an archive quota/export contract that preserves research evidence, and diagnose positive allocations with streaming offline statistics. Then preregister a fresh controlled-host operating-region study, retaining the 40 ms production and 300 ms evidence contracts unless a separately justified workload requires changes. Use at least the specification's 500 epochs per condition and a genuinely predeclared 10,000-epoch continuous test, independent randomized run units, verified pressure intensity, physical queue telemetry and failover faults. Prioritize normal versus 120 ms delay, worker capacity and storage failures rather than expanding arbitrary factors. Keep all negative Cycle 5 results. Do not proceed to trust retuning, learning, a second domain or isolation claims from these systems findings.
